Worth knowing

Evening and on-call pay 16% more

$39.27 against $33.85. Two evening hours are worth nearly two and a half day hours. If you get to choose, choose those. On-call especially — you're paid the higher rate for being available.

You pay no tax under $18,200 a year

That's about $700 a fortnight. Everything under it is yours in full, so don't ever turn down hours worrying about tax. Past that you only pay on the bit above.

Trips and long shifts are where the ground gets made up

One work trip is what paid the rent down. Put your hand up for those whenever they come around — they do more in one go than months of careful budgeting.

Subscriptions are the easiest cut

$30 a fortnight is $780 a year. At your hourly rate that's 23 hours of work. Worth checking what's actually in there.
