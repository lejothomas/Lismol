Sunday pays the most on paper. It isn't where I want you — keep your Sundays for church. If a Sunday shift is genuinely the only one going, take it and we'll talk, but don't build your roster around it.

Worth knowing

The rent help moves both ways

$140 a fortnight is the normal setting. When you’re earning well, tell me and I’ll drop it — that money is doing someone else good. When a fortnight goes wrong, tell me and I’ll put more in. Slide it here first so you know the number before you ask. Asking isn’t failing; it’s the whole arrangement working.

Tax has started coming out — that's normal

Tax is worked out as if every fortnight looked like that one. A big fortnight makes it look like you earn a lot all year, so more comes out. If your year total stays low, you get the extra back when you lodge your tax return. Lodge one every year, even if you think you earned nothing — that's how you get it back.

Super is yours too

Twelve percent goes into super on top of your pay. You can't touch it for decades, but it's real money with your name on it, and it grows the whole time. Check the fund has your tax file number so it isn't taxed extra.

Claim the first aid allowance every shift

It's small per hour but it's free money for a certificate you already hold. Make sure every shift is logged with it.

Big fortnights are for the loans, not for spending

When one lands, put a chunk straight at the loans and the emergency fund before anything else. That's what the last one did, and it's why the rent is clear.

Rent, food, insurance

The three where missing a payment has consequences you can't talk your way out of. If a fortnight is short, keep these, let the repayments wait, and tell me. That's information, not failure.
